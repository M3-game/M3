## Addendum — 2026-06-22 tablet-verses grid match (item c)

### What shipped

Tablet-verses board height restored from 10 → **12 rows** (v1.15 → v1.16)
so the playing grid matches Tablet Arcade exactly (12 rows × 10 cols at
50px tiles). One functional line changed: the `ROWS` constant. Scoring
formula unchanged (`moves × 300`).

### The audit behind it

Item (c) began as "tablet-verses should match tablet arcade; the user
believes the only difference is grid size — audit + align." The audit
(reading both live files) confirmed the hypothesis: tablet-verses was
forked from tablet arcade v11.11 and has since absorbed every arcade
change through v11.17 (the v11.13 end-confirm extension, the v11.14
terminology rename, the v11.16 stats blob, the v11.17 999 cap). The two
share the same tile set, palette, supernova/hypernova behavior, 1.5×
victory round, bonus-move caps, and slow-motion/history tooling. The
only **un-intended** difference was board height (10 vs 12). Everything
else that differs is **intentional memorize-mode design** — the rolling
passage text bar, chunk reveal, picker/level-select, the silent victory
round (no end-early prompt), and the `moves × 300` target formula
(vs arcade's random 3,500–6,500). Those were deliberately left as-is.

### Decisions

- **(c) supersedes verses TODO #13.** #13 ("port phone/sandbox Mech
  A/B/C/D enhancements into tablet-verses") points the wrong way for
  tablet. Tablet and phone verses are intentionally **different games**:
  the phone's smaller screen is dull without the enhanced mechanics
  (which is why phone got them), while the tablet version is engaging on
  the larger screen without them. So tablet-verses parity targets Tablet
  *Arcade*, not phone-verses. #13 is parked (note on `docs/verses/TODO.md`).
- **Fit risk accepted, to be playtested.** The 12-row board is ~108px
  taller; V-4 had dropped to 10 rows to fit a "standard/laptop
  viewport." User confirmed the target tablet has room; shipped at full
  50px tiles (truest "match arcade") rather than shrinking tiles or the
  text bar. Real-device fit check is the top playtest item.
- **Scoring left unchanged.** A taller board makes the unchanged
  `moves × 300` targets slightly easier. Not retuned — in verses, easier
  targets aren't a downside (the passage reveals win or lose; hitting
  target just adds the 1.5× round + stars). Revisit the multiplier only
  if playtest shows targets feel trivially easy.

### Scope boundary

Only tablet-verses changed. Phone-verses, phone-verses-sandbox, and the
two arcades were untouched — consistent with treating tablet and phone
verses as separate games.
